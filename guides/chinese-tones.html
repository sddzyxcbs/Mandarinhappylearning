Chinese Tones for Beginners

Why mā, má, mǎ and mà are four different words — and how to hear and say all four.

Mandarin is not hard because the grammar is complicated — it is hard because the same syllable at four different pitches means four different things. 妈 mā is mother, 麻 má is hemp, 马 mǎ is horse, 骂 mà is to scold. Get the tone wrong and the sentence survives; get it wrong consistently and people stop guessing. This guide covers what tones physically are, how to produce them, and the two or three changes that trip up every beginner in the first week.

What a tone actually is

A tone is a pitch contour across one syllable, not a musical note you hold. The pitch of your voice rises, falls, stays level, or dips and rises within roughly the time it takes to say one syllable. Mandarin has four of these contours plus a fifth, the neutral tone, which is short and depends on the tone before it.

Tones are written above the vowel in pinyin: ā á ǎ à. If you are reading Chinese text without tone marks, you are guessing.

  • 妈mā — mother — high and level
  • 麻má — hemp — rising, like a question in English
  • 马mǎ — horse — dips low then rises
  • 骂mà — to scold — sharp fall, like an annoyed 'no!'
  • 吗ma — question particle — neutral, short and flat

The four tones, one at a time

The numbers in brackets are the standard five-point pitch scale: 5 is the top of your comfortable range, 1 the bottom. You do not need to memorise them — they are just a way of writing down what your voice does.

1st阴平 yīn píngHigh and flat (55)Saying 'ah' at the doctor, held steady
2nd阳平 yáng píngRising (35)The 'huh?' you say when you didn't hear

4th去声 qù shēngSharp fall (51)A firm 'no'

Three tone changes you meet immediately

1. Two third tones in a row. The first one becomes a second tone. 你好 is written nǐ hǎo but pronounced ní hǎo. 我很好 (wǒ hěn hǎo) comes out wó hén hǎo.

2. 不 bù before a fourth tone becomes second tone: 不是 bú shì, 不要 bú yào. Before everything else it stays fourth: 不去 bú qù is wrong — it is bú qù only because 去 is fourth tone; 不吃 is bù chī.

3. 一 yī changes with what follows. Before a fourth tone it becomes second (一个 yí ge), before first, second or third tone it becomes fourth (一天 yì tiān), and when counting or at the end it stays first (一二三 yī èr sān).

Textbooks write the original tone; your mouth should not. If a native speaker repeats a word back to you with a different tone than you expected, this is usually why.

The mistakes English speakers actually make

  • Making the first tone too low. English speakers anchor 'level' at their normal speaking pitch. The first tone sits near the top of your comfortable range and stays there.
  • Turning the third tone into a deep dive. In connected speech the third tone is mostly just low and slightly creaky; the full dip-and-rise mostly happens when a word is said alone.
  • Confusing second and third. Both feel 'not flat'. The second goes up; the third goes down first. If you are unsure, exaggerate: rising vs. low-then-rising.
  • Skipping the neutral tone. 吗, 的, 了, 呢, 得 are short and unstressed. Pronouncing them with full tone makes you sound like you are reading a dictionary aloud.

How to practise without a teacher

Then move to two-syllable words, which is where real speech lives: 中国 Zhōngguó (1-2), 美国 Měiguó (3-2), 再见 zàijiàn (4-4), 谢谢 xièxie (4-neutral).

  • 妈 / 马mā / mǎ — mother / horse
  • 买 / 卖mǎi / mài — to buy / to sell
  • 问 / 闻wèn / wén — to ask / to smell
  • 书 / 树shū / shù — book / tree
  • 水 / 睡shuǐ / shuì — water / to sleep

Where tones fit in your first month

Do not try to master tones before learning words — that project never ends. Learn thirty high-frequency words with their tones attached, and let every new word carry its contour from day one. Tones are a habit, not a subject.

常见问题 · FAQ

Do I really have to learn tones?

If you want to be understood without effort, yes. Context rescues a lot, but a learner who ignores tones makes every sentence a small puzzle for the listener — and the listener usually switches to English rather than ask you to repeat.

How long until I can hear the four tones?

Hearing the four-way contrast usually takes a few weeks of daily listening. Producing them reliably, without thinking, takes a few months. The gap between the two is normal.

Are tones the same in Taiwan?

Taiwanese Mandarin uses the same four tones and largely the same system. Vocabulary differs, 儿化 is much rarer, and some neutral tones are pronounced more fully.

What is the fifth tone?

The neutral tone — short, unstressed syllables such as 吗, 的, 了, and the second syllable of 谢谢. It is not a fixed pitch; it follows the tone before it.
