Chinese Measure Words

You cannot say 'two book' or 'three coffee'. Every number needs a measure word — here are the twenty that cover most of daily life.

English has a few of these — two sheets of paper, three cups of coffee — but Chinese requires one before almost every noun, including nouns where English uses none. 'Two books' is 两本书, never 两书. The measure word is chosen by the shape, container, or category of the thing, and getting it wrong is understood but heard.

The structure

Number + measure word + noun. 一个人 (one person), 三本书 (three books), 两杯咖啡 (two coffees). The same slot is filled by 这/那 (this/that) and 哪 (which): 这个人, 那本书.

  • 一个人yí ge rén — one person
  • 三本书sān běn shū — three books
  • 这杯茶zhè bēi chá — this cup of tea

个: the default you will overuse

个 gè is the general-purpose measure word and it covers a huge range: people, problems, ideas, apples, companies. Beginners can survive on 个 alone. Native speakers use it constantly too — but they switch to a specific measure word when shape or politeness matters, and those switches are what you want to learn.

个 or 位 for people?

个 is neutral; 位 is polite and slightly formal, used for customers, guests, and people you are serving. 这位先生 (this gentleman) is respectful; 这个人 (this person) can sound dismissive depending on tone. In a restaurant you will hear 几位？ — 'how many (people)?'

Questions about quantity

几 jǐ expects a small number and needs a measure word. 多少 duō shao is open-ended and can drop it: 多少人 (how many people), 多少钱 (how much money).

  • 几个人？jǐ ge rén? — How many people? (expected answer under ten)
  • 多少钱？duō shao qián? — How much money? (open-ended)
  • 你要几个？nǐ yào jǐ ge? — How many do you want?

The mistakes worth fixing first

  • Dropping the measure word. 两书 and 三咖啡 are not 'casual' — they are ungrammatical.
  • Using 个 for everything in a shop. Understandable, but 一件衣服 and 一双鞋 are what a shop assistant expects.
  • Forgetting the tone change. 一个 is pronounced yí ge, not yī ge, because 一 before a fourth tone becomes second tone.

常见问题 · FAQ

Can I just use 个 for everything?

You will be understood, and beginners do it constantly. It sounds casual with books, clothing, and vehicles — 一本书, 一件衣服, 一辆车 are what people expect.

Do measure words change tone?

They follow normal tone sandhi. 一个 is pronounced yí ge because 一 becomes second tone before a fourth-tone syllable.

Which measure word is polite for people?

位 for customers, guests and strangers; 名 in formal lists and writing; 个 as the neutral default. Restaurant staff will ask 几位?

Is there a measure word for 'times'?

次 for occurrences (去过两次) and 遍 for complete repetitions of an action (再说一遍).
