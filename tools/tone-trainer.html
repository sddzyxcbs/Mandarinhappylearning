Press play, listen, then choose 1–4 (keyboard works too).

How to use it

  • Listen before you look. The character is hidden until you answer — guessing from the shape is not the skill you are training.
  • Ten minutes, then stop. Ear training degrades with fatigue; short daily sessions beat one long one.
  • Say it back. After each answer, repeat the syllable aloud. Recognition and production reinforce each other.
  • Watch the contour, not the number. The chart is what your voice actually does; the label 1–4 is just a name for it.

为什么第三声听起来不像第三声

Alone, the third tone dips low and rises. In speech, two third tones in a row turn the first one into a second tone: 你好 is written nǐ hǎo but said ní hǎo. This trainer plays single syllables on purpose — once you can hear the four in isolation, connected speech stops sounding like noise.
